### Changed

- Claims re-based on the published canon: the 14 Public Claims of *The Evolutionary Stress Framework: A Field Guide* v1.0 (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21937705, 17 Oct 2026) are the only numbered claim set, shown verbatim with their evidence-status label, boundary and published anchors.

Cooperative neurodiversity is a supplementary proposition (Terminology and Boundary Map wording), not a child of Public Claim 7.

Review status is **proposed** for all of them; none is approved, and the interface counts only approved links. The 128 v1.0 topic links are kept as history (19 marked as retired support readings).
- 'Evidence Layers 1-6' renamed 'Evidence Domains E1-E6' (ESF Constitution, Article III).
- Interface: Sources / Claims / Topics views; relation badges; approved/proposed tallies per claim; citation-corrected and annotation-revised markers; Errata and About panels; version banner.
